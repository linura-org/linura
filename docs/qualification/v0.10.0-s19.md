# v0.10 S19 qualification — BlueZ Bluetooth workstation experience

**Status:** Proposed acceptance contract; no BlueZ pairing/effect implementation or hardware support qualified.
**Target:** `arch-hyprland-v1`, declared dependency S16.
**Architecture:** [ADR 0049](../adr/0049-s19-bluez-bluetooth-authority.md), [S19 design](../../contracts/v010-s19-design.toml), [security qualification](v0.10.0-s19-security.md).

## Evidence ownership and source binding

S19 specializes Q9 native effect semantics, Q10 GUI/CLI/SDK convergence, Q11 maintained workstation, Q12 crash/recovery and Q13–Q14 security/inherited release gates. Every native case must bind the exact source/installed bytes, target BlueZ/kernel/runtime versions, boot, BlueZ unique D-Bus owner, adapter/controller and device identity/generation, authenticated caller/session, pending Agent1 challenge where applicable, native operation attempt and independently re-observed result.

Virtual HCI/VM fixtures may establish controlled BlueZ component or Level A mechanics only where actually exercised. They cannot prove the physical radio/RF environment, secure human-to-device confirmation, native HID/audio profile readiness or full Q11 qualification. BlueZ method replies, callbacks and video are not postcondition or security oracles.

## Managed and transient classification acceptance

External-effect classes and risk floors are trusted registration decisions, not client/provider hints. The proposed design intentionally treats all candidates as inactive managed effects. A future reviewed transient reclassification can occur only with proved unprivileged/no durable ambiguity/no stronger-than-`UserState` semantics, proper trusted exact plan, strictly newer post-effect evidence and durable attempt audit. It does not follow from the perceived simplicity of scan/stop/cancel.

For each activated managed operation require request, observe, deterministic canonical plan, validate/classify, authorize current principal/policy, durable prepare, exact narrow effect, independent verify, commit, audit and reconcile. Exercise success/no-change/denial/revocation/stale owner/expired challenge/duplicate request/native timeout/partial result/crash before and after dispatch/verification mismatch/restart with unknown effect. A timeout or error may have applied a BlueZ method; **never blindly repeat** Pair, RemoveDevice, trust or scan lease.

## Qualification levels

**A — automated exact-source Arch/BlueZ VM:** Genuine system-bus BlueZ with controlled native HCI fixture or explicit unavailable handling, ObjectManager/AgentManager/Device1 contract, adapter/device/lease/agent failure handling and deterministic independent oracles. A mocked BlueZ bus alone is unit/integration evidence, not native qualification.

**B — interactive VM:** Same pinned source and substrate; actual first-party shell/Agent1 presentation, focus, protected authentication, keyboard/pointer, screen reader, reduced motion, scaling, status/progress and no-AI manual paths.

**C — maintained physical workstation/Q11:** Independent nonvirtual evidence for real supported Bluetooth controller, scanning and device selection, authenticated pairing when supported, real passkey/confirmation, multiple devices and radio conditions, bond state across restart, reconnect and requested input/audio service profiles. Bind physical controller/driver/BlueZ version, boot/session/seat and exact-run digests; do not promote Q11 from virtual HCI or recorded video.
