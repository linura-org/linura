# Partnership program

Linura welcomes strategic relationships that improve the project's technical reach, qualification depth, hardware coverage, infrastructure capacity, research quality, or distribution without converting a partner into a project authority.

Partnership contact: `partners@linura.org`.

`partnerships@linura.org` is an alias for the same channel.

## Relationship boundary

A **partner** is not automatically a sponsor, investor, customer, maintainer, or governance participant.

The same organization may hold more than one relationship with Linura, but each relationship is documented separately. For example, an organization may be a Strategic Sponsor and a Hardware Partner while separately purchasing future enterprise support. None of those relationships grants merge, release, architecture, security, or governance authority.

Equity or financing conversations belong at `investors@linura.org`. Sponsorship belongs at `sponsors@linura.org`.

## Partnership categories

Linura uses relationship categories rather than prestige tiers.

### Technology Partner

An organization whose software, APIs, runtimes, or infrastructure integrate with Linura or provide a useful first-party interoperability surface.

Typical work:

- typed provider or capability integration;
- interoperability validation;
- reference architecture work;
- upstream issue coordination;
- joint technical documentation where appropriate.

### Hardware Partner

An organization that provides systems, components, devices, or engineering access for qualification.

Typical work:

- CPU/GPU/device qualification;
- workstation and server matrix coverage;
- firmware/driver evidence;
- reproducible acceptance on representative hardware;
- failure reporting and upstream escalation.

### Cloud and Compute Partner

An organization that provides cloud, GPU, CI, test, hosting, or lab capacity.

Typical work:

- disposable VM acceptance;
- architecture matrix execution;
- scale/reliability testing;
- build and release infrastructure;
- bounded test fleets.

### Qualification Partner

An organization that collaborates on evidence for a named environment or platform profile.

A qualification relationship must never be marketed as certification unless Linura has an explicit certification contract, criteria, evidence model, and authority boundary for that term.

### Distribution and OEM Partner

An organization that helps package, preinstall, distribute, or make Linura available through hardware, images, marketplaces, Linux channels, or managed environments.

Distribution does not create a private architecture branch by default. Public support claims must continue to match qualified evidence.

### Research and Security Partner

A university, research group, security organization, formal-methods group, or specialist engineering organization that contributes review, analysis, verification techniques, threat modeling, or research collaboration.

Security partners do not receive vulnerability access merely because of partner status. Access to embargoed security material follows `SECURITY.md` and explicit need-to-know authorization.

### Design Partner

A pre-commercial or early commercial organization that runs Linura in a bounded real environment and provides structured product/operations feedback.

Design-partner status is temporary and outcome-oriented. It does not guarantee roadmap priority, exclusivity, or permanent preferential treatment.

## What partners may receive

Depending on the relationship, Linura may provide:

- recognition on the Linura website;
- a public description of the collaboration;
- joint technical write-ups or qualification evidence;
- coordination meetings for the agreed integration or program;
- reasonable access to non-confidential project plans relevant to the collaboration;
- attribution for donated hardware, compute, engineering time, or research work.

## What partnership never grants

Partnership does not grant:

- architecture authority;
- roadmap vetoes;
- merge or release rights;
- security-policy exceptions;
- maintainer status;
- automatic sponsor recognition;
- endorsement by Linura;
- exclusivity unless a separate, explicit commercial agreement lawfully establishes a narrow exclusivity term.

## Evidence and claims

Any public claim such as *supported*, *qualified*, *verified*, *certified*, *recommended*, or *official partner* must match the actual evidence and agreement.

A logo exchange is not qualification evidence. A successful demo is not a support contract. Donated hardware is not architectural approval.

## Conflicts

Maintainers must disclose material partner relationships when they could reasonably affect a technical or governance decision.

Partner-contributed code, infrastructure, evidence, or documentation follows the same architecture, security, testing, review, and release requirements as other project work.
